Stock Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts a stockroom order with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the cases an order takes, the weeks a stock lasts, the shelves the cases need, and the cost a unit. It uses no named constants. The units, the case size, the weekly use, the case and shelf widths and the case price are yours to decide and read off your own count and supplier sheet. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your stockroom, your supplier or your budget and does not know whether the stock will really last, the shelves will hold the weight or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that an order is wise or a stock level is right. Every number here is your own entry.

The cases truth

Cases: the units divided by the units a case, rounded up, with the size of the last case.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the units on hand divided by the units used a week, with the units left over.

The space truth

Space: the cases a shelf takes side by side, the inches left over, and the shelves the cases need.

The cost truth

Cost: the cases times the case price, to the cent, and spread over the units.
